## Distinguish three hypotheses

1. **Target hypothesis:** a particular identity/state/interface violates a stated rule. Test against that target; level-3 output needs observed consequence and controls.
2. **Method hypothesis:** a testing strategy/helper improves useful discovery or reduces mistakes/effort. Compare fixed versions on matched cases and held-out families; finding count alone cannot establish improvement.
3. **Platform hypothesis:** a code/orchestration change improves execution/evidence/review. Test it as a separately versioned candidate; it cannot modify the trial's evaluator or authority to award itself success.

The records already support parts of these lifecycles; they do not establish a fully implemented general research platform. Existing workbench execution and effect predicates are narrowly tied to the owned practice app.
