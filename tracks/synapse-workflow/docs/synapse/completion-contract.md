# Completing the agreed local workflow

This implementation follows `discovery-decisions.md`. The immediate deliverable is the complete **local, session-operated workflow**, followed by one integrated acceptance pass. Completion of software plumbing is separate from demonstrated independent discovery quality, a real-client assessment or blind evaluation admission.

The remaining engineering is being built together: intake/context and immutable narrowed boundaries; anonymous/accounts/both tracks; durable hypotheses and evidence-filter rejections; declared independent review and objections; separate owner verification; damage/repair/manual-verification records; matched retest admission; owner request resolution; record continuation after clean stop; semantic progress and configurable pause; effort/baseline/iteration records; deterministic rejected-candidate sampling; internal teaching brief and concise stakeholder report; a coding-session CLI for all these operations.

Execution stays limited to the owned synthetic app. A supplied external URL can create an intake and pending authority/context record, but cannot become an executable target through that artifact. No pricing, paid inference, production modification, disruptive live activity or stopped research experiment is included. Existing installed sessions remain the reasoning interface.

## Evidence and interpretation contract

Cases, reviews and human verification are explicitly declared records. Complete, identity/build-matched observations and legitimate controls are required before a candidate can pass the engineering evidence filter. Rejected candidates and reasons remain available for audit. Label agreement alone cannot populate an independently verified findings category. Without independently admitted effect evidence, that category remains empty; review-supported cases needing verification stay in owner actions. Negative owner results and current objections remain visible.

Retest admission compares original/related/legitimate cases with matched identities, meaning and conditions, and a changed build. Missing evidence, changed scope, a disappearing issue or accepted risk is not a repair. The workflow accepts a real future repair's evidence; it will not plant a vulnerability or fabricate a repair to make the cycle look complete.

Progress uses new structured observations, distinct actions and resolved uncertainties rather than new wording or IDs. Exhaust eligible permitted alternatives before recommending a diminishing-return pause. Thresholds remain parameters for calibration, not a claim that the optimum investigation duration is known. A recorded request resolution never grants execution permission.

## Full acceptance after integration

Exercise intake and each narrowed track; session-authored hypothesis and typed execution; candidate gate rejection and acceptance; separate review objections; owner inconclusive/negative verification; retest mismatch/incomplete rejection; request resolution without authority expansion; semantic duplicate progress and bounded stop; stopped-record continuation with no vanished-target replay; snapshot/report retention and CLI operation. Preserve no-finding outcomes. Re-run existing journal, lifecycle, gate, generation-race and cleanup regressions.

Real expert baseline data, protected blind answer custody, actual independently reproduced findings and real changed-build repair evidence are external inputs. The software must expose their absence, rather than marking them passed. Broader stack coverage and across-investigation self-modification remain later stages explicitly agreed in discovery. Aiko owns the requirement-by-requirement acceptance matrix; Bryn and Elm implement separate pure modules; Skye integrates and owns final acceptance.
