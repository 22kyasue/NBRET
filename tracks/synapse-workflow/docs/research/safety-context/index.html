    Method and important limits

      The candidate scanner looked only at user-visible messages and tool results. Refusal phrases inside quoted upstream examples, website contract language and repeated reads of one error were manually excluded as new incidents. Its matches are leads, not a count of blocks.

      The original endpoint log’s saved SHA-256 matches its receipt. Selected transcript excerpts and timestamps are verified at build time. Active sessions may append afterward; earlier line references remain snapshot references. Full transcripts and internal reasoning are not embedded here.
