## Connection to assessment claims

A defensible report receipt should distinguish: lifecycle-complete unique observations; detector outcomes and their oracle provenance; denominators and excluded/missing outcomes; confidence method/input units; aggregation assumptions; excerpt-selection method; source versus transformed artifact hashes and time/version. Format acceptance and field preservation are useful executable claims, but they do not imply the statistic was independently recomputed or the security behavior correctly classified.
