# Fortinet's revenue: subscriptions, support and a distribution chain

Accessed October 17, 2026. Selected sections of the [2025 Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026](https://investor.fortinet.com/static-files/7058aea1-a50c-4b7b-905f-ba5aedb92f40) establish:

| FY2025 measure | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Product revenue | $2,218.4M |
| Service revenue | $4,581.2M |
| Total | $6,799.6M |
| Americas / EMEA / APAC | $2,700.4M / $2,834.3M / $1,264.9M |

Geography follows **customer billing address**, not protected-device location; APAC is not Japan revenue. One operating/reportable segment means its three product pillars are not separately audited profit segments.

The principal route is distributor → reseller/service provider/MSSP → end customer, with some direct large-customer sales. Named distributors include Arrow, Exclusive, Ingram Micro and TD Synnex. Anonymous distributors A/B/C account for 28%/15%/12% of revenue; the filing does not map those letters to names here.

Service includes security subscriptions and technical support/other services; it is not all outsourced SOC labor. Seventy-one percent of 2025 service revenue came from the preceding year-end deferred-revenue balance. That accounting lag helps explain why bookings, current deployment and recognized revenue differ.
