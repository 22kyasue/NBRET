# A partner contract changes who performs response

**Read 2026-10-17:** all nine pages of [BT's October 2024 Falcon XDR service-description schedule](https://business.bt.com/content/dam/bt-business/pdfs/terms-and-conditions/service-schedules/security/Managed%20CrowdStrike%20Falcon%20XDR%20Service%20Schedule%20Part%20B_Oct2024.pdf), including the final response-target table. This is **BT's offering**, not direct Falcon Complete terms or Japan-wide practice.

The customer installs sensors and supplies connectivity; BT configures/optimizes the platform and commissions it. Customer acceptance testing has a five-business-day period, with deemed-acceptance provisions.

Foundation reports incidents and leaves mitigation to the customer. Foundation Plus adds analyst recommendations while customer implementation remains explicit. Premium Eagle-i describes policy-aligned automatic containment; customer remediation precedes reinstatement. Changes use authorized requests; complex work requires scope/quote authorization.

Reporting varies: twice yearly, quarterly or monthly. Standard raw telemetry and incident-log retention are seven days; longer raw retention is optional. These records must not be conflated with six-month service-request visibility.

P1 initial acknowledgement targets 15 minutes; this is not completed containment. The separate 99.9% portal-availability target has exclusions, while update/restoration times are targets. Premium P1/P2 support requires an available customer employee.

The Order and other schedule parts remain unread. No purchased contract, actual customer outcome, AI model implementation or universal service guarantee is established.
