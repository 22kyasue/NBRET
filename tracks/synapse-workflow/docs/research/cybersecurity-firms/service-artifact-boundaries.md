# What public service pages establish about operations and promises

**Read 2026-10-17.** This checkpoint separates advertised service behavior from the exact enforceable terms of a signed engagement. A marketing statement that mentions an SLA is evidence of that statement; it is not the SLA schedule itself.

## LAC JSOC: notification, device care and reporting

The [English JSOC page](https://www.lac.co.jp/english/service/operation/jsoc.html), read fully, explicitly advertises 15-minute notification/protection for critical incidents as SLA guaranteed. It describes analyst discovery/judgment/notification, countermeasure advice, policy tuning, 24/7 device health checks, urgent trouble reporting/restoration support, and monthly portal summaries. These establish a broader operating lifecycle than simply forwarding alerts.

The page does not supply the signed severity definitions, clock start/stop rules, exclusions, service credits or exact authorization to execute each countermeasure. The [Japanese-source dossier](lac.md) records an analysis-start boundary; that must not be rewritten as a universal 15-minute ingestion-to-containment guarantee. The English page says 2.5 billion logs/day while the newer Japanese material gives a different volume. They are distinct dated/source claims, not interchangeable measurements or proof of inconsistency in the deployed platform. The English page has no clear content revision date in the capture.

A representative quiet shift would include device-health review and portal/report work alongside alert analysis. During an incident, discovery and severity judgment precede notification and countermeasure guidance. This is a reconstruction from advertised service tasks, not an observation of LAC's internal shift handover or a customer's case.

## NRI Secure: shared SOC and individually priced scope

The [shared log-monitoring page](https://www.nri-secure.co.jp/service/mss/log_monitoring), read fully, says it correlates logs from more than 200 product/service types, uses AI for triage/initial analysis/severity judgment, and combines this with 24/7 analysts. It advertises Japanese/English support from Japanese/U.S. SOC sites and more than 80 analysts centered on experienced/certified members. Product support is explicitly subject to separate confirmation.

Pricing is by individual quotation, varying with licenses, users and implementation duration/content. The page does not publish a universal ingestion allowance, response clock, monthly deliverable specimen, binding data-residency clause or customer-specific escalation/containment authority. “Necessary response” is too broad to establish that every service tier authorizes autonomous device isolation. The [AgenticBlue dossier](nri-secure.md) separately distinguishes currently documented initial analysis from future expansion into automated response.

## Current limits

No statement here substitutes for an actual signed SOW (statement of work: the agreed tasks and exclusions), SLA (service-level agreement: measured commitments and remedies), data-processing agreement or specimen report. Those remain explicit retrieval targets. Buyer-authored accounts are still needed; a quote selected and hosted by a vendor is evidence of that quote, not independent verification of typical outcomes. No contact forms or gated-download submissions were used.

The [BT schedule](bt-falcon-service-schedule.md) now provides a public partner scope/acceptance/change/reporting example. It does not fill the missing signed Japan/customer-specific terms.
