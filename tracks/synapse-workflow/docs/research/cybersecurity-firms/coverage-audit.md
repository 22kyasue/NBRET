## Evidence-class audit

- Corporate and financial claims retain dates, legal units and period/perimeter labels. Parent totals, security categories, ARR and consolidated/standalone accounts are not equated.
- Provider-authored manuals establish documented behavior. Public contracts are versioned examples. They do not establish a customer's signed order, configuration or achieved result.
- Buyer-authored policy/troubleshooting is distinguished from vendor-selected testimony. Buyer-hosted consultant reports retain their authorship.
- Reconstructed days/incidents are explicitly explanatory inferences. Real RCA/status artifacts are identified separately.
- Rules/statistical detection, generative assistance, tool-based investigation and executable automation remain separate mechanism classes.
- Source files have hashes and complete/partial/unread boundaries. Reading tests is not running them. A local wrapper is not the remote engine.
- Exact models, privacy, authority, completion, rollback and prices are unknown where the selected evidence does not establish them.

## What “reviewed” means here

A named source's recorded main text or explicit line range has been read; unvisited links, image-only details and whole repositories are not credited. The catalog includes discovery and failed retrieval as distinct states. The source ledger and receipts take precedence over an overview's shorthand.

This package supports learning and comparison within those boundaries. Further studies can pursue newly available contracts, public implementations, independent outcomes or additional product branches; the continuation record preserves those questions without representing their answers as established.
