## Organic development versus acquisition

An acquisition can add people, customer contracts, intellectual property, distribution or a product. It does not establish that the purchased technology was integrated, retired or profitable. Conversely, an internally developed current feature can use acquired foundations. The dossiers therefore attach dates and legal status to individual events rather than label an entire company “organic” or “acquisition-driven.”

For Palo Alto's Japan operations, the 2019 article additionally records Nagoya office 2015, Osaka 2016 and a Japanese Unit 42 team in 2016. Its customer/partner counts are historical snapshots, not October 2026 counts. Other reviewed company histories have different granularity; unresolved origin details remain explicit gaps.

## Source quality caveat

A search-generated description of an old graphical Cisco timeline mismatched dates and events. It was not used to populate this chronology. The readable contemporary 1992 subsidiary release supplies the local milestone instead. Footers and modern product navigation on historical pages are not evidence those products existed at the original publication date.
