## Why a subscription can still buy people

A billing/accounting category and a delivery category are different. A security subscription can include an operated service; professional-services revenue need not contain every hour of managed security work. Hardware-backed security subscriptions, technical support, incident consulting and 24/7 analyst operation must therefore be compared using documented offerings/contracts rather than labels alone. Exact recognition rules require the respective annual-report notes.

No currency conversion, same-period adjustment, organic-growth decomposition or market-cap estimate was performed. Acquisition growth can differ from organic growth; period changes and partial-year consolidation must be resolved before comparing growth rates. Missing standalone results remain missing, rather than estimates copied from parent totals.
