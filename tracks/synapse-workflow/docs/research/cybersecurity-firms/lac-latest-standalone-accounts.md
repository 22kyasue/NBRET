# LAC's latest standalone accounts: services are not all subscription revenue

Accessed October 17, 2026. LAC's [public-notice index](https://www.lac.co.jp/corporate/info/public_notice.html) lists its nineteenth-period accounts, published June 16, 2026, and explicitly records the October 1, 2025 merger with KDDI Digital Security. This is later completion evidence; no registry extract was inspected.

The [eight-page notice](https://www.lac.co.jp/corporate/pdf/notice_19.pdf), extracted text read completely, covers April 1, 2025–March 31, 2026. **Standalone**, not consolidated: revenue ¥54,643M; operating profit ¥2,462M; net profit ¥2,532M. These figures cannot replace the differently scoped group anchor in [financial anchors](financial-anchors.md).

Its accounting policy distinguishes consulting/assessment work recognized as obligations progress (or at completion for short work), monitoring recognized evenly across contracted service periods, and third-party products/maintenance recognized at shipment or service commencement. System integration and hardware/software resale are separate activities. Therefore total sales are neither pure security sales nor recurring managed-security subscriptions.

The related-party note identifies KDDI as direct 100% owner and reports ¥4,843M revenue from it. It gives no security-only split, customer concentration outside named related parties, operational SLA, analyst staffing or AI margin. Accounting recognition explains economics; it does not prove service quality.

Reading boundary: all eight pages of extracted text, including accounting policies, related parties and subsequent-event note. No visual layout verification or audit opinion was inferred.
