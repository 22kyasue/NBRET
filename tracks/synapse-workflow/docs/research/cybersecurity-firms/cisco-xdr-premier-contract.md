# Cisco XDR Premier: delegated containment has a written boundary

Accessed October 17, 2026. The [13-page service description](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en_us/about/doing_business/legal/service_descriptions/docs/Cisco-XDR-Premier-Services.pdf), version 1.0, February 1, 2024, was read as extracted text. It is historical public contract evidence, not proof of a current customer's entitlement.

Activation validates customer-installed integrations, API access and playbooks. The customer maintains licenses, connectivity, contacts and context. A customer-approved response playbook specifies identification/containment actions, supported components and permission per event or action type. Cisco investigates and executes that authorized subset; other remediation remains customer work or guidance.

Incident tickets have one-year rolling retention; this is not raw-telemetry retention. Sharing incident data with a partner needs customer authorization.

The engagement objective is contact within 30 minutes after P1 prioritization, 45 for P2, where no recommendation already exists: monthly 95% on-time objective, starting measurement 60 days after activation. It is neither detection latency nor containment time; no financial liability follows a miss under this clause.

Remote Talos response/proactive work consumes separate service hours, expires without rollover and may stop when exhausted. Proactive scope/deliverables require approval. Product technical support and general implementation are excluded. Root-cause identification and complete detection are not guaranteed.

Reading boundary: all 13 extracted pages, including duplicated priority matrix and exclusions. No executed contract, regional addendum, visual inspection or production outcome was reviewed.
