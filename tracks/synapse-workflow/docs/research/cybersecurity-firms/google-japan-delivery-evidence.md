# Google security in Japan: education and expertise are different from a contract

Accessed October 17, 2026. Google's [March 12, 2025 Japan Cybersecurity Initiative announcement](https://blog.google/intl/ja-jp/company-news/technology/cybersecurity-initiative/), main text read completely, records its Japanese cybersecurity research hub opening in March 2024.

It describes work with the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry's small-business outreach, planned free training during the first half of 2025, threat-intelligence/expertise sharing with participating organizations, a limited certificate-training allocation and an industry/government/academic expert forum. Mandiant contributes frontline knowledge within that initiative.

This establishes a dated Japan-facing training/research and knowledge-sharing presence. It does **not** specify a Japanese incident-response contracting entity, number or physical location of responders, Japanese-language emergency coverage, local data residency or an enterprise response SLA. Program announcement is not verification of later training completion or participant outcomes.

The global Mandiant workload figures in the announcement are not Japan revenue or staffing. Research-hub presence must not silently become a claim that every Japanese customer receives locally staffed, locally processed managed defense.
