Work covered November 2023–September 2024, totaling 97 working days across ten consultants, including reverse engineers.

The division of labor matters: Mandiant assessed, recommended and reassessed; 3CX remediated. This is scoped consultancy, not continuous monitoring or proof the consultant deployed fixes.

A chronology remains unresolved. Page three says all critical/high findings were mitigated or remediated by January 10, 2024, while page two includes later testing and an April 2024 critical-issue remediation. Do not silently correct that date or infer a clean all-current-versions certification.
