# Falcon's public master terms do not specify every managed-response permission

Accessed October 17, 2026. The [public master terms](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/legal/terms-conditions/) were read completely through Exhibits A–C. No effective date was visible; another valid agreement or expressly superseding order can govern. This is document analysis, not advice about a customer's legal rights.

The terms distinguish Falcon Complete/OverWatch product-related services from separately scoped professional services. Installation is customer work unless installation services are purchased. Professional-service time/material estimates are not guaranteed completion dates.

Exhibit A describes collected machine events, scripts, binaries, logins, commands, URLs/network data and optional suspicious-file analysis, potentially including personal data. Customer data is confidential, while defined non-identifying execution metrics/threat-actor data are excluded from that category. The stated purposes include cross-customer protection and product development; this does not establish any particular generative-model training practice.

Termination references purchased retention periods; destruction permits specified compliance/back-up retention. No uniform number of days or Japanese residency commitment was established here. Linked data-protection terms remain separate and unread.

Customer-contractor access can require customer revocation or a support request, with a stated 72-hour outer limit for support-assisted disablement. **That clause concerns third-party contractors**, not proof of Falcon Complete's action approval policy. An executed managed-response authority matrix remains unreviewed.

Follow-up: the current [DPA landing](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/legal/data-protection-agreement/) states conditional applicability and replacement of prior data-processing agreements. Its linked PDF was inaccessible through the reading tool; no PDF clauses were read, and no alternate route was attempted.
