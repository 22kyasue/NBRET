# LAC ownership: control, full ownership and operating merger are separate dates

Accessed 2026-10-17. Selected LAC sections of KDDI's [FY2025 corrected securities report](https://www.kddi.com/extlib/files/corporate/sustainability/governance/risk-management/pdf/yuho_2025_TC5t58.pdf), submitted March 31, 2026, establish:

| Milestone | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| January 22, 2025 | KDDI obtained control: ownership increased from 31.59% by 60.84 percentage points to 92.43% |
| By March 31, 2025 | Subsequent share-sale-demand procedures brought ownership to 100% |
| Acquisition accounting | ¥33,204M consideration includes ¥11,349M fair value of the earlier stake plus ¥21,855M cash; not all new cash |
| Post-control consolidation | ¥11,744M LAC revenue and ¥1,637M profit recognized after control through March 31; not full-year LAC sales |

Goodwill of ¥16,405M represents expected excess earnings/synergies in acquisition accounting; it is not deployed technology value measured independently. Do not substitute KDDI group revenue for LAC security revenue.

Reading boundary: cover/correction context; section 5 “important contracts” page 56; LAC acquisition note pages 123–125. The 247-page filing remains partial. Accounting corrections elsewhere were not audited by this research.

Separately, LAC's [July 17, 2025 merger announcement](https://www.lac.co.jp/news/2025/07/17_press_01.html), read completely, names October 1, 2025 as the planned merger date with KDDI Digital Security, LAC surviving. The later [public-notice index](https://www.lac.co.jp/corporate/info/public_notice.html) explicitly records the merger as completed on that date; no registry extract was checked. See the [latest standalone accounts](lac-latest-standalone-accounts.md) for the subsequent accounting perimeter. It describes the 2018 joint venture's networking/security origin and the intended combination of monitoring, incident and global capabilities, not measured integration success.
