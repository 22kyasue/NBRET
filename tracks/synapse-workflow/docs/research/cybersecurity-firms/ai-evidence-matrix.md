## Evidence questions still open across the sample

Most reviewed numerical claims are provider benchmarks, selected customer assessments or undisclosed-method outcome claims. None of this investigation's code reads establishes independently measured production effectiveness. The [Microsoft trial methodology](microsoft-phishing-trial.md) is now reviewed with limits preserved. The [GMO Flatt blackbox benchmark](takumi-blackbox-benchmark.md) is now read: its planted-issue sample, confidence thresholds and unequal time budgets limit generalization.

For every action-taking feature, the next high-value evidence is the exact tool/identity scope, policy decision, approval record, remote completion signal, failure state and recovery path. For every “AI” feature, distinguish training, retrieval, inference, optional improvement use, stored customer records and audit retention. These terms must not be collapsed into a single privacy checkbox.

Later feature evidence: [AgentiX control/retention](pan-agentix-controls.md), [Google architecture boundary](google-architecture-boundary.md), [Splunk action-taking Guided Response](splunk-guided-response.md), [FortiAnalyzer query/inference path](fortianalyzer-ai-privacy.md), [Takumi benchmark](takumi-blackbox-benchmark.md) and [future domestic inference](takumi-domestic-inference-announcement.md) refine the earlier overview cells. Exact feature/version scopes take precedence over portfolio-level shorthand.

Additional evaluation: [Microsoft DTDA](microsoft-dtda-evaluation.md) separates online alert feedback from an offline repeated case sample; neither establishes autonomous containment.

Additional Google client mechanisms: [conversational assistant and preference writes](google-gemini-client-source.md); [natural-language translation followed by search](google-natural-language-search-source.md). These are distinct from TIN and do not establish generated-rule deployment or endpoint remediation.

[Splunk Triage](splunk-triage-controls.md) adds a distinct version-scoped investigator: selected detections/findings, frontier-model-only constraint, per-batch/daily coverage limits and explicitly selected connector actions. General assistant hosted-model choices do not apply to this path.
