# Takumi domestic inference: announced, with a future start date

**Read 2026-10-17**, complete release text of the [issuer's 2026-10-05 announcement](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000078.000027502.html).

The paid domestic-inference option is announced for **2026-11-02**, covering whitebox, blackbox, graybox, Autofix and AI pentesting. It describes Japan-hosted inference infrastructure with model/infrastructure/pricing individually proposed and supply constraints. It asserts assessment inputs remain in Japan under the option.

As of this research date, this is **announced future availability**, not verified general availability or a retroactive change to the default service. ZDR flexibility and quality parity are provider claims, not disclosed independent evaluations. Exact contract/model selection remains unknown.

The [October 16 Japanese security manual](https://shisho.dev/docs/ja/c/security/) was also read in full and matches the general overseas-inference/non-guaranteed-ZDR boundary in the [canonical dossier](gmo-flatt-security.md). Its note that special arrangements may relax constraints should not be read as automatic entitlement for every customer.
