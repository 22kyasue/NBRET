# Cisco MDR: a historical service mandate and measurement boundary

**Read 2026-10-17:** all 16 extracted pages of the [version 6.0 service description](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en_us/about/doing_business/legal/service_descriptions/docs/managed-detection-and-response.pdf), modified **2022-12-01**. It predates Splunk's acquisition and does not establish today's universal Cisco offer.

Activation configures the MDR platform, aligns runbooks, connects customer APIs and tests collection/response. The customer supplies tokens, connectivity, contacts and required administrative access. Product licenses/support are separate.

Cisco monitors, correlates, investigates and recommends. With customer permission it performs approved changes on covered components, limited to documented playbooks. Outside-scope remediation stays with the customer; optional incident-response services have separate terms.

Incident tickets have one-year retention; underlying product logs have separate policies. Third-party sharing requires customer authorization.

The engagement measure is **30 minutes after P1 prioritization, 45 for P2**, for unresolved cases without an existing recommendation; its service level is **95% on-time engagement**. It is not ingestion-to-containment. Measurement starts 60 days after activation, with exceptions, customer-response holds and capped credits.
