## Stopping boundaries for a selected source

Read its meaningful workflow end to end, including named local helpers and available tests; record unreviewed dependencies. Stop when the selected boundary is understood, rather than crediting every repository file or cited paper as read. Closed code, restricted documents and unspecified contracts remain unknown. A separate source is needed to change those states.

Blocked/rejected URLs are recorded and are not revisited through another route. No-result searches are bounded discovery outcomes, never proof that evidence does not exist. A historical brochure, announced feature, current manual and production evaluation remain distinct evidence classes.
